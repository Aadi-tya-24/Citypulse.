        Find my area & commute

        Uses your browser's location, only to find the nearest area below. CityPulse never stores or shares your location.

        Report an issue

        No names, phone numbers or addresses are collected, only the area, the issue type and the time. A single report is logged but changes nothing; CityPulse only reacts once about 15 reports of the same problem, in the same area, arrive within 5 minutes. This prevents one person (or spam) from triggering a false alarm.

        Why do the numbers change?

        Real data: temperature, wind, rain and air quality come from Open-Meteo. They change only every few minutes or hours, like real weather.

        Demo data: traffic speed, complaints and bus/train delays are made-up (there is no free live feed for them). They drift slowly on their own so the page feels alive, and they jump when you press a demo button.

        The score moves whenever any of these move. Each item is tagged "real data" or "demo data" under Data sources.
